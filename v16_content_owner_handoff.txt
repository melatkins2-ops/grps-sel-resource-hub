GRPS SEL Resource Hub — Content Owner Handoff

PURPOSE
The hub should remain a practical decision-support resource, not become a static collection of links.

RECOMMENDED OWNERSHIP ROUTINE
- Monthly: refresh Quick Tools.
- Quarterly: test high-use GRPS and external links.
- Twice yearly: review Support a Scholar and MTSS pathway content.
- Annually: review CASEL/PBIS/Attendance Works framework language.

RESOURCE ADDITION STANDARD
Before adding a resource:
1. Confirm it solves a clear user need.
2. Tag it to all appropriate audiences.
3. Use the exact resource title whenever possible.
4. Prefer a direct tool/document over a broad landing page.
5. For attendance/MTSS supports, confirm it is an intervention or an actionable problem-solving tool—not merely an action such as outreach.
6. Confirm the URL is public and usable from a district-managed browser.

KEEP THE HOMEPAGE SIMPLE
Do not add new homepage sections unless they improve a common user task. New content should usually be placed in a resource center, the finder, or a support pathway.
